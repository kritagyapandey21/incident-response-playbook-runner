# TR-011: Volumetric DDoS Against Public Services

**Base severity:** 3 (High)
**IOCs:** none

## Summary
UDP reflection and SYN flood attacks saturate bandwidth of public web services, causing
outages. Traffic originates from thousands of spoofed or botnet sources.

## Behaviours
Traffic flood, service unavailable, bandwidth saturation, UDP amplification, SYN flood.

## Recommended Containment
1. [contain] enable_ddos_scrubbing | host | Route traffic for {target} through DDoS scrubbing provider
2. [recover] review_capacity | host | Review autoscaling and rate limits for {target}
