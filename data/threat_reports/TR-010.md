# TR-010: Internet Port Scanning and Reconnaissance

**Base severity:** 1 (Low)
**IOCs:** 103.145.13.77

## Summary
Internet-wide scanners continuously probe perimeter addresses with sequential TCP SYN
port scans. When dropped by the firewall these are background noise, but they indicate
which services are exposed.

## Behaviours
Port scan, sequential TCP SYN, horizontal scan, reconnaissance, firewall drop, probing
many ports from a single source.

## Recommended Containment
1. [contain] block_ip | ip | Add scanner {target} to perimeter block list
2. [recover] review_exposed_services | host | Review services exposed on {target}
