# TR-002: Credential Phishing via Lookalike Login Pages

**Base severity:** 2 (Medium)
**IOCs:** micros0ft-login.example, it-support@micros0ft-login.example, Password expires today

## Summary
An ongoing credential-harvesting phishing campaign sends emails impersonating IT support
with subjects such as "Password expires today". Links lead to lookalike Microsoft / OWA
login pages hosted on typo-squatted domains. Victims who click and submit credentials
have their mailbox accessed within hours, enabling business email compromise.

## Behaviours
Phishing email, user clicked link, lookalike login page, credential harvesting, user
submitted credentials, typosquatted sender domain, mailbox takeover, business email compromise.

## Recommended Containment
1. [contain] reset_credentials | user | Force password reset for {target}
2. [contain] revoke_sessions | user | Revoke all active sessions and refresh tokens for {target}
3. [contain] block_domain | domain | Block phishing domain {target} at mail gateway, proxy and DNS
4. [eradicate] purge_emails | email_sender | Purge all messages from sender {target} from every mailbox | min_severity=High
5. [recover] scan_host | host | Run full EDR scan on {target} to check for follow-on payloads
