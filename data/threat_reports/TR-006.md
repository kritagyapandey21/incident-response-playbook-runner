# TR-006: Data Exfiltration over DNS Tunneling

**Base severity:** 3 (High)
**IOCs:** exfil-cdn.example

## Summary
Adversaries exfiltrate data from internal servers by encoding it in DNS queries: very
large volumes of TXT or NULL record lookups with long, high-entropy subdomains are sent
to an attacker-controlled authoritative domain. Database servers holding customer data
are the most frequent source.

## Behaviours
DNS tunneling, high-entropy subdomains, excessive TXT queries, data exfiltration over
DNS, large outbound DNS volume, database server beaconing over DNS.

## Recommended Containment
1. [preserve] capture_pcap | host | Capture full packet capture of DNS traffic from {target}
2. [contain] block_domain | domain | Sinkhole tunneling domain {target} on internal resolvers
3. [contain] isolate_host | host | Isolate {target} from external networks
4. [eradicate] shutdown_service | host | Take the database service on {target} offline until the exfiltration path is removed | min_severity=Critical
5. [recover] rotate_credentials | host | Rotate database and service credentials on {target}
